Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data.

How do circles work?
Bridges organizes your contacts into three circles based on closeness. Each circle has its own reminder rhythm, so you're prompted to reach out to your closest friends more often than your wider circle.

How do I plan a hangout?
Create a hangout plan, propose a few time and activity options, and share the link with your friends. Once everyone votes, Bridges recommends the best option automatically.
