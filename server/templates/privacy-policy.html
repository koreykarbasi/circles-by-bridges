Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Bridges ("we," "us," "our") helps you maintain and grow your personal relationships. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile information: your name and profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Contact information you enter: names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, birthdays, notes, interests, and relationship labels for the people you add to your circles. This may include information about people who do not use Bridges themselves.
  • Hangout planning data: titles, descriptions, proposed times/activities, and votes submitted by you or people you invite (including invitees who do not have a Bridges account, identified only by the name they enter).
  • Device information: a push notification token, if you enable notifications, and your device timezone, used only to deliver timely reminders.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate the app: to show you your circles, remind you to reach out to people, plan hangouts, and let you log in securely. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to operate Bridges:

  • Resend, to deliver transactional emails (such as password resets).
  • Apple, to deliver push notifications and to support Sign in with Apple.

These providers only receive the minimum information needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

Your Rights

You can edit or delete any contact or hangout you've created at any time within the app. You can delete your entire account and all associated data at any time using the Delete Account option in Settings → Profile.

Children's Privacy

Bridges is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
