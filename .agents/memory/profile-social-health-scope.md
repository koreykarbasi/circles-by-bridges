---
name: Profile social health scope
description: User-defined scope of the health score and its placement.
---

Social health belongs on Profile only. Circle 2 and Circle 3 contribute; Core Circle, Acquaintances, snoozed contacts, and contacts without a usable last-contacted date do not.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose these exclusions and requested a reusable compact component for a possible future Home version, not a Home addition now.

**How to apply:** Preserve these product boundaries in future score changes. A compact rendering option alone is not authorization to place the card on Home or expand the scored circles.
