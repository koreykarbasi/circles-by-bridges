Bridges App
Purpose:
Helping Adults maintain and grow the relationships they care about
To help users maintain authentic relationships by organizing people into “emotional proximity” circles (based on Dunbar’s social brain theory) and delivering timely prompts, reminders, and nudges to keep bonds strong.
Core Concept: Dunbar's Circles Framework
Circle 1 (5 people): Closest people (daily/weekly contact: partner, best friend, parents)
Circle 2 (10): Trusted confidants (weekly/monthly)
Circle 3 (35): Strong acquaintances (monthly/seasonal contact)
Circle 4 *Not in MVP* (100): Broader social network (rare, but relevant touchpoints)

Main functionality:
Reminds you of your friends birthdays
Reminds you when you haven't spoken to someone
Reminds you to text people with intentional prompts
Reminds you to call people with intentional Prompts
Helps you set up hangouts by sending surveys to your friends
Helps you set up hangouts by recommending activities
Helps you set up hangouts by recommending dates and sending calendar invites
AI: access to messages, checks how long ago you talked to someone
AI: enter contacts into circles
AI: Based on interests and messages suggest text, call conversations starters, and activities to do while you hang out.

1. Reminders

2. Suggestions
People to call and text based on last interacted with, interests, and circle level.
More prompt examples will be mentioned in the prompt section below but here’s some examples.
Prompt Examples:
“Ask how their marathon training is going 🏃‍♂️”
“They started a new job in June—check in!”
“It’s been a while since you talked to Maya. Want to send a voice note?”
Reminders, Suggestions and Notifications based on Circles:
Circle 1:
Birthday reminders (1 month, 2 weeks, 5 days before & Day of).
Prompts like: “Tell X what you appreciate about them today” or “Plan something big for their birthday.”
Nudge: “Check in weekly with Y.”
Circle 2:
Birthday reminders (2 weeks, 1 day before).
Prompts like: “Share something you admire about Z” or “Send them a voice note.”
Nudge: “Haven’t connected in a month, drop them a line.”
Circle 3:
Birthday reminders (day-of only).
Prompts like: “Ask them how their week has been.”
Nudge: “It’s been 3 months since you last talked—reach out?”

Prompts:
Prompts vary across circles (e.g., appreciation, admiration, curiosity questions).
Examples:
Circle 1/2: “Tell X what you appreciate about them.”
Circle 1/2/3: “Tell Y something you admire about them.”
Circle 3: “Share an article/meme related to their interests.”

CIRCLE 1 (Core People: ~1–5)
Goal: emotional intimacy, appreciation, and regular meaningful contact.
Additional Prompts:
“Tell [Name] something you deeply appreciate about who they are.”
“Tell [Name] one way they’ve helped you grow.”
“What’s something [Name] does that makes you feel safe?”
“Send [Name] a reminder of one of your favorite shared memories.
“Ask [Name] what’s been weighing on them lately.”
“What’s something you admire about how [Name] handles challenges?”
“Leave [Name] a voice note telling them why they matter to you.
“When’s the last time you made [Name] laugh?”
“Send them a ‘just because’ message: no reason, just love.”

CIRCLE 2 (Close/Trusted Friends: ~10)
Goal: trust, warmth, subtle acts of care, and gentle reconnection.
Additional Prompts:
“Tell [Name] something you admire about how they live their life.”
“Remind [Name] of a time they made your day better.”
“Send a message: ‘I’ve been thinking about you lately — how’ve you been?’”
“Share something new you’ve learned or done lately and invite [Name] to join you.”
“Tell [Name] you’re proud of them for something (big or small).”
“Ask [Name] what’s bringing them joy right now.”
“Plan a micro-hangout: a walk, coffee, or phone call.”

CIRCLE 3 (Light Friends / Acquaintances: ~50)
Goal: stay warm, occasionally reconnect, open the door for future depth.
Additional Prompts:
“Tell [Name] something you admire from afar — a quality or habit.”
“React to their recent story/post with something thoughtful.”
“Send [Name] a relevant article, song, or meme that reminded you of them.
“Check in: ‘Hey, it’s been a minute — want to catch up sometime soon?’”
“Ask what’s new in their world and actually listen.”

CIRCLE 4 (Weak ties / dormant network: ~150)
Goal: stay lightly connected and visible.
Additional Prompts:
“Congratulate [Name] on a recent milestone or life event.”
“Comment meaningfully on their latest post.
“Forward an opportunity that might interest them.”
“Send a brief check-in once every 6–12 months.”

Universal Occasional Prompts (Optional for Any Circle)
“What’s a compliment you haven’t said out loud to [Name] yet?”
“Which friend would love to hear a random thank-you from you today?”
“Who’s overdue for a celebration? Send some encouragement.”
“What’s a conversation you’ve been putting off that could bring you closer?”
