Privacy Policy for Bridges

Last updated: [DATE]

Bridges ("we," "us," "our") helps you maintain and grow your personal relationships.
This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT
- Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure
  one-way hash, never in plain text).
- Profile information: your name and profile photo, if you choose to add one.
- Contact information you enter: names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos,
  birthdays, notes, interests, and relationship labels for the people you add
  to your circles. This may include information about people who do not use
  Bridges themselves.
- Hangout planning data: titles, descriptions, proposed times/activities, and
  votes submitted by you or people you invite (including invitees who do not
  have a Bridges account, identified only by the name they enter).
- Device information: a push notification token, if you enable notifications,
  and your device timezone, used only to deliver timely reminders.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We use your information solely to operate the app: to show you your circles,
remind you to reach out to people, plan hangouts, and let you log in securely.
We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
We use the following service providers to operate Bridges:
- Resend, to deliver transactional emails (such as password resets).
- Apple, to deliver push notifications and to support Sign in with Apple.
These providers only receive the minimum information needed to perform their
function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

DATA STORAGE & SECURITY
Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and
are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

YOUR RIGHTS
You can edit or delete any contact or hangout you've created at any time within
the app. You can request deletion of your entire account and all associated
data by [contacting us at koushyar23@gmail.com / using the Delete Account
option in Settings]. [Update this line once the in-app delete flow ships.]

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Bridges is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
